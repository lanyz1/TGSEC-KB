# WhatsApp Account Management Guide

> Source: TG @xinghe1999 — WhatsApp蓝白标和普号养号攻略
> Condensed reference for cross-border e-commerce sellers

## 1. Account Registration & Preparation

- Use **clean residential IPs** (avoid datacenter/机房IPs)
- Prepare 1-10 IPs for rotation depending on account count
- Use **AI tools** to generate registration materials and scripts
- Register on **fresh/clean devices** — avoid devices previously flagged by WhatsApp
- One device, one account ideally; use app clones if necessary

## 2. IP Management (Critical)

- Check IP purity with: `ifconfig.me`, `ip.sb`, `ping0.cc`
- Recommended: **1-2 accounts per IP** max
- **Residential IP > VPS datacenter IP** — WhatsApp flags datacenter ranges aggressively
- Switch IP immediately if the current one gets blocked
- Keep IP geographically consistent with the account's claimed location

## 3. Device Environment

| Device | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **iPhone** | Generally cleaner, easier to manage proxies (v2ray/Proxy etc.) |
| **Android** | More flexible, app cloning available, but easier to fingerprint |

- Different accounts → different app instances or app clones
- Avoid cross-account contamination of device fingerprints
- Keep OS and WhatsApp app updated

## 4. Account Nurturing (养号) — The Core

### Critical Period: First 24 Hours
- Do NOT send mass messages on day one
- Simulate **real human behavior**: scroll, check status, reply slowly
- Add 1-2 contacts max on day one

### 7-15 Day Nurturing Timeline

| Day | Activity |
|-----|----------|
| 1-3 | Browse statuses, send 2-3 personal messages, set up profile completely |
| 4-7 | Gradually increase activity: add 3-5 contacts, reply to messages |
| 8-15 | Normal usage pattern, still no bulk marketing |
| 15+ | Gradually introduce business messaging, monitor for restrictions |

### Daily Hygiene
- Change profile photo/status periodically (shows real-person behavior)
- Engage with status updates from contacts
- Don't send the same message to multiple contacts rapidly
- Use **template messages** for testing before scaling

## 5. WhatsApp Business Blue/White Badge

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Blue Badge (蓝标)** | Official verified business account — high trust, requires Facebook Business Manager |
| **White Badge (白标)** | Standard business account — less verification, easier to get |

### Requirements for Blue Badge:
- Complete business profile (all 4 elements filled)
- Linked **Facebook Business Page**
- Business documents ready for review
- Account in good standing with no recent restrictions
- Review time: ~24-48 hours
- **Facebook Page association is strongly recommended** — improves both verification success AND account stability

## 6. Facebook Integration & 2FA

- Linking a Facebook Business Page increases account weight and security
- **Enable 2FA (Two-Factor Authentication)** on every account
- Use **Google Authenticator** for 2FA management
- Save **Cookie/session data** for recovery
- 2FA + IP need to be coordinated — switching devices triggers re-verification
- FB binding also helps in ban recovery — support can cross-reference

## 7. Ban Appeal Templates

### Template A — New Account Banned Before Use

> Hello WhatsApp Support,
>
> I have not even started using my new account properly, but it has already been banned. I believe this may be a mistake.
>
> This account is important for my business customer service and Facebook promotion. Please help me review the account and remove the ban if possible.
>
> Thank you for your help.

### Template B — Re-banned After Unblock

> Hello WhatsApp Support,
>
> My account was just unblocked recently, but after I logged in again, it was banned once more.
>
> I have not done anything wrong and did not have time to use it normally. This account is connected to customer service and Facebook promotion, so the ban has affected my business communication.
>
> Please review my account again and restore access. Thank you.

### Template C — Restricted During Normal Use

> Hello WhatsApp Support,
>
> My WhatsApp account has been restricted again while I was using it normally. I believe this may be a mistake.
>
> This account is important for communicating with customers. If I cannot contact them, it will affect my business and Facebook advertising progress.
>
> Please review my account carefully and remove the restriction. I would really appreciate your help.

### Template D — Multiple Restrictions

> Hello WhatsApp Support,
>
> My account has been restricted multiple times, and I would like to ask for a careful review.
>
> I use this account for normal customer communication and business support. I did not intentionally violate WhatsApp rules. Some customers may have misunderstood my service or reported the account by mistake.
>
> Please help me restore my account access. Thank you for your time and support.

## 8. General Safety Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| No rapid adds | Don't add more than 5-10 contacts per day early on |
| No bulk broadcast | Test with template messages before scaling |
| Profile freshness | Update avatar/status/bio periodically |
| Device limit | Max 3-4 accounts per device, ideally 1-2 |
| IP + device + behavior | All three must be **consistent** — a US account on a US IP using US-timezone behavior |
| Avoid triggers | Don't send links too early, don't send the same text to many people |
