- Consumer companion apps: ~$120M total 2025 → ~$200M reasonable 2026 (Appfigures).
- Companion apps cumulative downloads: 220M by mid-2025; H1 2025 downloads +88% YoY (Appfigures).
- Replika: 40M+ registered; €5M GDPR fine.
- Caryn AI: $71,610 in one beta week at $1/min (2023).
