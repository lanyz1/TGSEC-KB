## The chain (pipeline vocabulary)
mining (ore) → concentrate → separation/refining (oxides) → **metallization** (oxide→metal/alloy) → magnet manufacturing (NdFeB, SmCo) → end products (EV, wind, robotics, defense).
Recurring taxonomy: "mine-to-magnet" integration; tolling (fee-for-processing, client keeps ownership); offtake (承购); price floor; DFARS compliance; a "non-China chain" means every step outside PRC territory AND without PRC technology.

## Magnets & elements
- **NdFeB** (Nd-Pr-Fe-B): EV traction motors, wind, robotics; Dy/Tb grain-boundary doping for heat resistance.
- **SmCo** (samarium-cobalt): stable 300–550°C → aerospace/defense (missiles, radar, fighter jets). **Samarium metal is the defense chokepoint.**
- Tracked elements: Sm, Dy, Tb, Gd, Y (medium/heavy); La, Ce, Pr, Nd (light). Adjacent chains: tungsten, gallium, antimony.
- Terms: REO/TREO, "separated oxide", magnet wire (권선/hairpin), actuator.

## Samarium metal process (industry standard)
- Lanthanothermic (metallothermic) reduction + vacuum distillation: Sm2O3 + La/misch metal, ~1200°C, 10^-3–10^-4 Pa, tantalum crucible, dendrite condensation (~99.9% Sm), 20–40 kg/batch, ~90% recovery; molten-salt electrolysis is the alternative route.
- Scale math: ~100 kg/furnace/day → 240 t/yr ≈ 7–15 furnace array — use to sanity-check announced capacities.

## Policy landmarks
- **US DFARS 252.225-7052**: from Jan 2027, bans China-origin magnets in defense procurement down to raw-material traceability — THE trigger driving all non-China investment. Companions: DLA stockpile buys, USTR critical-minerals negotiations, G7 alliance target (dependence <60% by 2030).
- **China export controls**: Apr 2025 (7 medium/heavy REEs) → Oct 2025 (extraterritorial 0.1%-value rule + technology export ban incl. SmCo/NdFeB) → suspended to 2027-01-10 under the truce; entity-list blacklisting of MP Materials/USA Rare Earth etc.; pattern: keeps upstream metals/compounds tight while letting finished magnets flow. Supply-side tightening: first-ever mining-quota cuts, imported + by-product ore brought under total-volume control, mining-rights approval moved to the national ministry, six-Central-SOE whitelist, smelter curbs, stockpiles, and enforcement actions against foreign firms' export violations — net effect: export volume down / value up (magnet-export shift).
- **Vietnam**: raw-ore export ban effective Jan 2026 — amended Geology & Minerals Law added a dedicated REE chapter (special strategic-mineral class; state-designated operators only; exploration/mining licences gated at prime-minister level; explicit clause encouraging international technology transfer for separation/refining/deep processing); national REE strategy being drafted; export limited to deep-processed products (single oxides/metals/alloys/powders/magnets; must be on the PM list AND meet the industry-ministry standard); ore rights stay state-held; foreign investors must partner + process locally; Dong Pao partner trials under way but auction still pending; state pilot lines (Hoa Lac 100 kg/h; Phu Tho trial). Pre-reform export reality: oxides ~95% shipped via Hong Kong (i.e. transhipped into China), metals ~50% to Japan — the "HK channel" is what the deep-processing rule aims to cut; black-market ore leakage via Lao Cai persists.
- **EU**: CRMA strategic projects (Carester etc.); courting Vietnam for tungsten/REE/gallium; internal docs flag the LS-Lynas Vietnam chain as competitive risk.
