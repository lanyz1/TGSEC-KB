# Telegram 官方申诉英文模板（2026-08 实战产出）

用途：telegram.org/support 表单 + @smstelegram（X/Twitter）私信，各发一份。
适用：全设备掉线 + 旧号失效（无会话可登录）的最后尝试。
核心筹码 = **恢复邮箱**（能证明邮箱所有权才有戏）+ 用户名 + 设备历史。

```text
Hello, I have permanently lost access to the phone number registered to my Telegram account (the SIM has been cancelled and the number is unreachable — I cannot receive SMS or calls on it). I am logged out on all devices and cannot log back in because the login code goes to that number.

My account has Two-Step Verification enabled and I still control the recovery email: [你的恢复邮箱]. I can prove ownership of this email address.

Details I can provide: old number: +60 [旧号] / username: @[用户名] / approximate registration date / devices I last used.

Please advise if there is any way to regain access or at least secure my account from self-destruction while I try to recover the SIM through my carrier. Thank you.
```

注意：
- 表单字段：Please describe your problem / Your full legal name / Your email / Your phone number → Submit
- 官方回复口径大概率是"找回 SIM"，别把期望吊高；但附恢复邮箱的申诉有极少数成功案例
- 与运营商复活旧号并行推进，不要只等申诉
