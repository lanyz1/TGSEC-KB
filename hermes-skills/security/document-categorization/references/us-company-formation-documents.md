# US Company Formation Documents — Content Signatures

Common document types found in U.S. (especially Colorado/科州) company formation packages.

## 1. Articles of Incorporation (公司注册证书)

**Source**: State Secretary of State (e.g., Colorado SOS)

**Signature phrases**:
```
"Articles of Incorporation for a Profit Corporation"
"OFFICE OF THE SECRETARY OF STATE OF THE STATE OF COLORADO"
"CERTIFICATE OF DOCUMENT FILED"
"Entity ID #"
"profit corporation"
"registered agent"
"authorized to issue" + "shares"
```

**Document # format**: `20261851771` (year + sequential)
**Entity ID**: same or separate number

**Chinese name**: `科州公司注册证书` or `公司章程（注册版）`

---

## 2. Certificate of Good Standing (公司良好存续证明)

**Source**: State Secretary of State

**Signature phrases**:
```
"CERTIFICATE OF FACT OF GOOD STANDING"
"in good standing with this office"
"Secretary of State of the State of Colorado"
"formed or registered on"
"has complied with all applicable requirements"
```

**Chinese name**: `公司良好存续证明`

---

## 3. IRS CP575A — EIN Assignment Notice (EIN税号通知书)

**Source**: IRS (Internal Revenue Service)

**Signature phrases**:
```
"Notice CP575A" or "CP575A"
"Employer ID Number"
"EIN is"
"Internal Revenue Service"
"Department Of the Treasury"
"We assigned you an employer identification number"
"42-" (EIN prefix for Colorado-originated)
"Form 1120" (corporate tax return)
```

**EIN pattern**: `XX-XXXXXXX` (e.g., `42-3804288`)

**Chinese name**: `EIN税号批准通知书` or `CP575A税号通知`

---

## 10. Registered Agent Consent (注册代理人同意书)

**Not typically a PDF — often a JPG/PNG scan or signed form.**

**Context clues**:
- Name of a registered agent service (e.g., "Yufeng Services LLC", "US CO RA LLC")
- Signature of agent representative
- Accompanies Articles of Incorporation filing

**Chinese name**: `注册代理人同意书`

---

## Typical Package Contents

A standard Colorado / Delaware INC formation package usually includes:

| # | Document | Chinese Name |
|---|----------|--------------|
| 1 | Articles of Incorporation | 公司注册证书 |
| 2 | Certificate of Good Standing | 公司良好存续证明 |
| 3 | IRS CP575A (EIN) | EIN税号通知书 |
| 4 | Corporate Bylaws | 公司章程 |
| 5 | Stock Certificate | 股票证书 |
| 6 | Register of Members | 成员登记册 |
| 7 | Certificate of Incumbency | 在职证明 |
